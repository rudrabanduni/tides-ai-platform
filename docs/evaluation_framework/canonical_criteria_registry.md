## 1. Registry Architecture & Methodology

This registry defines the authoritative criteria for evaluating startup applications at TIDES IIT Roorkee. It directly mirrors the structure and requirements of the **LaunchIndex Startup Incubation Application Pitch Deck** (18 slides across 6 evaluation domains plus cover metadata).

### Key Methodological Rules
1. **Template Integrity**: Criteria, wording, and requirements originate directly from the pitch deck specification. No arbitrary weights or cutoffs are introduced without institutional authorization.
2. **Conditional Evidence**: Fields such as prototype demos (`PS-10`) are evaluated conditionally—the absence of an operational prototype for early-stage/deep-tech ventures is evaluated based on laboratory progress and next build milestones rather than an automatic failure.
3. **Multi-Founder & Strict Factuality**: Full support for multi-founder teams is mandatory (`FT-01` through `FT-06`). Commercial claims strictly distinguish signed pilot contracts from unsigned letters of intent (`BC-10`), and paying customers from trial prospects (`BC-08`).
4. **IP Non-Discrimination**: The absence of a registered patent (`II-07`) is never treated as an automatic lack of innovation; trade secrets, execution speed, and proprietary algorithms are credited appropriately.
5. **Original Slide Comparison Notice**: *The original `.pptx` template is not stored locally in the workspace; criteria definitions represent the authoritative LaunchIndex specification provided in the system requirements. Exact slide-by-slide formatting must be periodically cross-referenced against the master presentation template.*
